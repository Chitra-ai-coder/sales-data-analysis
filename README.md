SALES DATA ANALYSIS
AI Academia Internship | Chitrabhanu Hazra | October 2026

ABOUT
Analysis of retail sales data covering trends, top products,
regional performance, and ML-based sales prediction.

TOOLS USED
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Scikit-learn, SciPy, Joblib

DATASET
The Superstore dataset contains 9,994 rows and 21 columns of retail transaction records spanning from 2014 to 2017. It captures order metrics, sales, profit, discounts, customer segments, and product categories across United States regions.

KEY FINDINGS
1. The Technology category generates the most total revenue, exceeding $800,000, which is roughly 36.4% of the company's total gross sales.
2. March yields the highest average sales per individual order at roughly $295, significantly higher than the annual baseline average of $229.86.
3. The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier dominates as the top product generating over $61,599 in sales, while everyday office supplies like Eureka Disposable Bags sit at the very bottom, generating only $1.62.
4. By using a non-linear Random Forest Regressor, our predictive R-squared score improved to 0.7198, fixing the negative score generated by standard Linear Regression.
5. Our Gradient Boosting Classifier achieved 91.70% accuracy, proving mathematically that Profit and Discount are the two strongest features for classifying a cart as High Value (>$54.49).
